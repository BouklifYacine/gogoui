About seventy new components: toasts, a confirmation dialog, form fields bound or not to TanStack Form, a data table with search, sort, selection and pages, list filters, page layout, settings rows, slide to confirm, file and photo drops, and a guided tour. Also Alert, Accordion, Toggle, ToggleGroup, NumberField, ScrollArea, StatCard and a ThemeToggle that keeps the theme on the device. A `color` prop (neutral, primary, info, success, warning, danger) on Button, Badge, Alert, Progress, Spinner, Switch, Checkbox, Radio, Slider, RangeSlider, Toggle, ToggleGroup, Avatar, StatCard, PillTabsList, StepBars and EmptyState; Button and Badge gain the solid and subtle shapes. New tokens give readable words on each colour's fill.
